Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Paperbacks & hardcovers

  • We ship printed books (paperback and hardcover) within India only. Readers outside India can buy on Amazon.
  • Shipping costs a flat ₹80 per order, and is free on orders of ₹999 or more.
  • In-stock books are dispatched within 3–7 working days. Pre-orders are dispatched on launch.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–10 working days after dispatch, depending on your PIN code.
  • You'll get the courier name and tracking number by email when your order ships.

E-books

  • E-books are delivered digitally — no shipping. Once payment is confirmed, your download link appears on your order page and in your email.
  • Pre-ordered e-books are delivered by email on launch day.
  • Each e-book can be downloaded up to 5 times. Need more? Just ask.

Problems with delivery

If your parcel hasn't arrived within 14 working days of dispatch, contact us with your order number and we'll trace it or send a replacement.
