What's the difference between Moolank and Bhagyank?

Moolank (root number) comes from the day you were born and describes your nature and instincts. Bhagyank (destiny number) comes from your full date of birth and describes the path your life tends to take. A good reading looks at where the two agree and where they pull apart.

How is my Personal Year 2027 worked out?

Add your birth day and birth month, add 11 (for 2027: 2+0+2+7), and reduce to one digit. Born 14 August: 1+4+0+8 = 13, 13 + 11 = 24, 2+4 = Personal Year 6. The Complete Life Report and Life Blueprint include a month-by-month forecast.

Will I have to change my name legally?

No. A corrected spelling is used first on your signature, email, business cards and social profiles. Changing official documents is optional.

Which name system do you use?

Name numbers use the Chaldean letter values, as in Vishal's books. The free calculator on this page uses the same values.
