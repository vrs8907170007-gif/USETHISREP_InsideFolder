VRU EntertainmentCelebrity / Corporate Events & Productions

Vishal's event career began at sixteen, under the mentorship of Showbiz, where he worked on televised award shows and brand launches through the late 1990s and 2000s. He later floated his own company, VRU Entertainment Pvt. Ltd. — the events and production arm associated with Mangal Murti Limited, where he served as CEO of the Events Division. Together these years span nearly two decades of award shows, brand launches, and press events, working with national broadcasters, global brands, and Bollywood's biggest names. Below is a curated slice of that portfolio, drawn directly from VRU's own work-profile records.
