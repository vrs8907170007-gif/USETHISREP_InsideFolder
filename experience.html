VRU Entertainment — Industry Context
2009 – 2013 outlook

Operating inside a fast-growing industry

VRU's parent, Mangal Murti Limited, operated within an Indian media & entertainment industry sized at INR 584 billion in 2008 and projected (FICCI/PwC) to reach INR 1,052 billion by 2013 — with the filmed entertainment segment alone worth an estimated INR 117 billion in 2010.
