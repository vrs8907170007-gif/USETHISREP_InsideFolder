Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it's used. We collect only what we need, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Orders: name, email, mobile number and (for printed books) delivery address — to deliver your books and send receipts.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Razorpay. We receive a payment reference and status, never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Messages, bookings and sign-ups: what you type into forms (contact, stay requests, pass reservations, Talk2Me) — to reply to you.
  • Talk2Me accounts, comments and likes: your name, email and what you post, stored with Google Firebase.
  • Your browser: your cart and recently entered details are kept in your own browser's storage so you don't have to retype them. We don't use advertising trackers.

Who processes it

Razorpay (payments), Netlify (website hosting and order records), Google Firebase (Talk2Me, comments), FormSubmit and Brevo (sending emails). Each processes data only to provide its service.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes. You can ask us to delete your Talk2Me account, comments or messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us. You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove your saved cart and details.
