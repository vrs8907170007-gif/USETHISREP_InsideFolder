Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Cancelling an order

  • Pre-orders (e-book, paperback or hardcover) can be cancelled for a full refund at any time before delivery.
  • Printed books that are in stock can be cancelled for a full refund until they are dispatched.
  • To cancel, email or WhatsApp us with your order number.

E-books

Because e-books are delivered instantly as digital files, they can't be returned once downloaded. If your file is faulty or doesn't open, contact us within 7 days and we'll send a working copy or refund you in full.

Paperbacks & hardcovers

If your book arrives damaged, misprinted or is the wrong title, tell us within 7 days of delivery (a photo helps) and we'll replace it or refund you in full, including shipping. We don't otherwise accept returns of paperbacks that were delivered as ordered.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay within 2 working days of approval. Your bank may take a further 5–7 working days to show it. If a payment failed but money was deducted, it is reversed automatically by the bank.
