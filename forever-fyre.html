What Forever FYRE is

Forever FYRE is a festival and recurring event series set on the 65-acre fruit orchard grounds of Pandora City — ten days of live music, nature, adventure, and wellness, built around two mega stages and multiple zones. It closes with a New Year's Eve grand celebration designed to be the country's biggest.

Every edition is eco-friendly and zero-plastic, with an RFID cashless wristband covering access, payments, food, and activities across the entire site.

Festival zones & the site map

Ten numbered zones run the length of the grounds: the Main Stage Arena, VIP Lounge, a multi-cuisine Food Court, four stay zones (Jungle Tent Experience, Couple Luxury Tents, Music Lovers Dome, Luxury Villa Zone), a Wellness & Relaxation area, a Market Place, a Sports & Fun Zone, an Adventure Zone, and full Transport & Parking infrastructure.
