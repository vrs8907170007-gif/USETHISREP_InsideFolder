The idea, plainly stated

Pandora City takes 65 acres of working land and layers hospitality on top of it without replacing it. Every fitness credential, every hour spent training abandoned dogs into a veterinary instinct, every year of hosting guests — all of it converges here, split into five sub-farms that each stand on their own financially, and together form one destination.

The land is held in the names of Ramnayan Singh and Usha Singh; Vishal leads the project as Founder, having run it under V Agro since 2021.

The Five Sub-Farms
  • Fruit Farm Retreat — orchards & jungle stays
  • Goat Farm — Katoo supply & Eid special
  • Dairy Farm — cows, buffaloes & milk centre
  • Adventure Park — 40+ outdoor/indoor activities
  • Fish Farm Retreat — aquaculture & water activities

A 20-acre orchard integrated with jungle-stay tourism — Mango, Guava, Papaya, Lemon, Chikoo, Coconut, Grapes, Drumstick and Banana across the plantation, with 1,000 couple tents, 10 Machaan stays, and 10 Tree Villas built into the orchard itself. Guests move through fruit picking, nature walks, bird watching, cycle trails, organic farming, and farm-to-table dining.
